# tokprof-source-opencode

tokprof's source of OpenCode (`opencode-ai`) sessions. OpenCode writes no log files: it keeps every session in a SQLite store.

- **Where:** `$XDG_DATA_HOME/opencode`, by default `~/.local/share/opencode`, also on macOS. The stores are `opencode.db` and `opencode-<channel>.db` (a channel other than `latest`, `dev`, `beta`, `next` and `prod`); `$OPENCODE_DB` names one more, relative to that directory unless absolute. `opencode-next.db`, an earlier 2.x store that 2.x imports into `opencode.db` and leaves there, is not read. A store is opened read-only and never written; SQLite reads the `-wal` journal OpenCode writes first.
- **A session is a log:** known by the store and its id, of the size of its rows (`octet_length` of `data`). Its last write is the latest `time_updated` of the session and its messages; its last activity the latest `time_updated` of its messages; its first activity the session's `time_created`. Given as a target, a store means its main session written last.
- **Two layouts, often in one store:** opencode 1.x writes `session` (`id`, `parent_id`, `directory`, `title`, `agent`, `time_created`, `time_updated`), `message` (`id`, `session_id`, `time_created`, `time_updated`, `data`) and `part` (the same, with `message_id`). opencode 2.x writes `session_v2` (the same columns, plus `fork_session_id`) and `session_message` (with `type` and `seq`), and copies the 1.x sessions there under the same ids. A session in both is read from `session_v2`; its messages are the union of both tables by id, the `message` row kept when both have it, since 2.x's copy drops the usage of compaction calls. A column an older release lacks reads as null.
- **Requests:** a 2.x `assistant` message with non-zero `tokens`; a 1.x message with `role` `assistant` gives one request per `step-finish` part (with its own `tokens` and `cost`), else one from its own `tokens`. `tokens.input` is uncached input, `cache.read` read, `cache.write` written with the short lifetime (OpenCode asks for the provider's default), and `output` plus `reasoning` the output: `reasoning` comes on top of `output` (1.19.3: `total` = `input` + `output` + `reasoning` + `cache.read` + `cache.write`). The model is `model.id` (2.x) or `modelID` (1.x), after its last `/`. `providerID` `anthropic`, or a model starting `claude`, gets Anthropic's cache rules; `openai`, or a model starting `gpt-`, OpenAI's; any other none. A request is sent at `time.created` (1.x: its `step-start` part) and ends at `time.completed` (1.x: its `step-finish` part).
- **Compactions:** a 2.x `compaction` message with `status` `completed` is a marker of the kind of its `reason` (`auto`, `manual`); its `tokens`, completed or failed, are a compaction call on its `model`, else the latest request's. A 1.x user message with a `compaction` part is a marker (`auto` true: `auto`, else `manual`), and the steps of the assistant with `summary: true` are compaction calls. The request after a compaction call is marked.
- **Subagents:** a session with a `parent_id` is a subagent log of the topmost session above it in the store, as the `task` tool starts one. Its chain is `subagent: <title>` without ` (@<agent> subagent)`, and that is its task.
- **Forks:** 2.x copies the messages of the session it forks under new ids with their own times; messages older than the session are left out, as OpenCode's own stats do.
- **Own cost:** the sum of `cost` over the requests and compaction calls, compared with tokprof's price in `tokprof summary`, never used as a price. Free models such as `big-pickle` have no price.
- **Following:** `tokprof ui` watches the data directory and polls the store and its `-wal` every 2 s. A change gives the main sessions of the store written in the last hour; a session in a store is read whole each time.
- **Not read:** the JSON files of opencode before 1.x (`storage/`); prompts, tools, triggers, hand-offs, version-control actions, share names; `session_part` (2.x content); title-generation calls, which OpenCode stores per session only.

Seen in opencode 1.18.16 and 2.2.4 (the author's store, the free `big-pickle` model) and 1.19.3 (a scratch session with tool steps and an `explore` subagent). The compaction rules come from opencode's source and its 1.x-to-2.x migration (main of 2026-11-10): no store at hand holds one. `tests/fixtures` holds synthetic stores as SQL text: a 1.x store with tool steps, an aborted reply, a compaction and a subagent; and a store with both layouts, a session 2.x copied and went on with, a 2.x compaction, and a fork.
